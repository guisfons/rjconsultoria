## 1. Project Overview

RJ Consultoria is a bespoke WordPress theme designed and developed for a business consultancy firm. The theme provides a clean, professional institutional web presence with fully manageable content via Advanced Custom Fields Pro, allowing the consultancy team to update their services, team profiles, and thought leadership articles without technical assistance.

## 2. The Problem

Business consultancies rely heavily on projecting authority, expertise, and trust. A generic template often undermines these qualities by looking indistinguishable from competitors. The client required a digital presence that felt custom-tailored to their brand identity, with an easy-to-use backend that didn't overwhelm their non-technical staff with complex page builder interfaces.

---

## 3. The Solution & Architecture

A custom WordPress theme engineered for performance and ease of use, utilizing ACF Pro to create a structured, fill-in-the-blanks editing experience for the client.

### Architecture

- **ACF-Driven Content:** All major page sections (hero, services, team, testimonials, contact) are mapped to programmatic ACF field groups.
- **Custom Page Templates:** Specialized templates for specific content layouts, ensuring the design remains intact regardless of the content inputted.
- **Modular Components:** Extensive use of `get_template_part()` to keep the codebase DRY and maintainable, especially for recurring UI elements like service cards and team member profiles.

---

## 4. Technologies Used

- **CMS & Backend:** WordPress 6.0+, PHP 8.0+, MySQL
- **Content Management:** ACF Pro
- **Styling:** Custom CSS/SCSS with a mobile-first approach

---

## 5. Design Process & UI/UX

The design prioritizes a corporate, trustworthy aesthetic suitable for a B2B consultancy. Typography is legible and authoritative, and the color palette aligns with the firm's branding guidelines to instill confidence. The user journey is streamlined to guide prospective clients from understanding the firm's expertise directly to a consultation inquiry.

## 6. Project Outcomes

- **Brand Authority:** A tailored digital presence that reinforces the firm's professional image.
- **Client Autonomy:** The consultancy team can independently manage their content updates, reducing ongoing maintenance costs.
- **Performance & SEO:** A lightweight theme structure ensures fast load times, contributing to better search engine visibility.
